Stringless

Interface parts that explain themselves without a single word on them.

Each piece below is live, and each was built with the Stringless skill and its runtime. Move toward a control, hover it, focus it with Tab, hold it, press it. Press ? inside a piece to see the words it keeps for screen readers.

Blotter

save · light · strangeness 3

What is it
A rocker blotter beside a handwritten note.
What it touches
Only the paragraphs changed since the last save, which are still wet ink.
What it will do
It rocks toward the page as you approach; the wet ink glistens harder.
What it costs
One press, or Ctrl+S.
Where it is in time
Dry ink is saved. The felt keeps a mirrored imprint of the last save.

Ember corner

delete · heavy · strangeness 3

What is it
A charred corner on each card, with a warm seam.
What it touches
Its own card.
What it will do
Hovering starts the char. Holding spreads the burn; letting go early cools it.
What it costs
A full hold, or two presses from the keyboard.
Where it is in time
The card falls as ash. The ash pile cools for seven seconds, and pressing it restores the card.

Frost pane

private · light · strangeness 3

What is it
A pane of frosted glass parked at the photo's edge.
What it touches
The photo it's mounted on.
What it will do
Hovering slides it over a third of the photo, so you see exactly how others will see it.
What it costs
One press, or Space.
Where it is in time
Private means frosted. You can still wipe a clear patch, because it's yours; it frosts over when you leave.

One marble

choose · light · strangeness 3

What is it
A wooden tray with three wells and a single glass marble.
What it touches
The order total below it.
What it will do
Hovering another well tips the tray and the marble starts to roll toward it.
What it costs
A click, or arrow keys. Native radio buttons sit underneath.
Where it is in time
There is one marble, so there can be one choice. The well it left keeps a ring of dust for a moment.

Vapor

AI suggest · light · strangeness 3

What is it
Mist that gathers after your cursor when you pause.
What it touches
Your text, exactly where the words would go.
What it will do
Hovering starts to condense it. Mistier words are the ones the model is less sure of.
What it costs
Tab condenses it into ink. Escape blows it away. Typing the same letters takes them one by one.
Where it is in time
Vapor isn't yours yet. Accepted words land as wet ink and dry.

Slot print

export · medium · strangeness 3

What is it
A printer bar under a report, and a rack of paper stocks.
What it touches
The report. The loaded stock picks the format.
What it will do
Hovering feeds the print halfway out of the slot in its real future form: a page, a ledger strip, a photo.
What it costs
One press. The real file is made and downloaded.
Where it is in time
The finished print drops into the tray and stays there as a link to the file.
