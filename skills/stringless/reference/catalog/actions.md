# Catalog: actions

Starting concepts, not templates. Roll first (`scripts/roll.mjs`), choose what fits the product's world in LEXICON.md, and push past the entry. Strangeness in brackets. Each entry gives rest, rehearsal, commit and echo in short form.

## Save
- **Blotter** [3]. Unsaved paragraphs are wet ink. The blotter rocks toward the page on approach; pressing rocks it across the wet text, which dries. The felt keeps a mirrored imprint of the last save. See `examples/wet-ink-save.html`.
- **Dry-down** [1]. Keep the existing button. Unsaved content has a faint wet sheen that pulses while the button is hovered; saving dries it.
- **Seal** [3]. A wax tile beside the document. On hover the wax softens and the spot where it will land brightens; pressing stamps it on the corner. Editing later cracks the seal, which shows unsaved changes.
- **Kiln** [4]. Drafts are soft clay that dents under the pointer. Saving fires them: the color deepens and a glaze appears. Editing softens them again.
- **Anchor** [4]. Unsaved work floats and drifts a little. Saving drops an anchor; the content settles with a thud and stops drifting.

## Delete
- **Ember corner** [3]. A charred corner on the item. Approach brightens a seam; hover starts the char; holding spreads it; release cools it. The item falls as ash, and the ash pile is the undo. See `examples/ember-delete.html`.
- **Eraser** [2]. For drafts and provisional marks. The control is an eraser end; the rehearsal lightens the content; commit rubs it out and leaves crumbs, which restore it.
- **Hairline** [3]. For fragile, temporary things (previews, cached files). A crack starts at the touch point on hover, holding spreads it, commit shatters it. The shards stay for a few seconds and reassemble when clicked.
- **Let go** [4]. Items hang from pins. Hovering a pin frays its thread; holding snaps it and the item drops to a pile along the bottom edge, where it can be picked back up until the pile is swept.
- **Tide** [4]. Water creeps up the item's base as you hold, ink lifts and blurs, and the item dissolves. A puddle stays where it stood.

## Archive
- **Drawer** [2]. A drawer lip below the list. Hovering it pales the item and opens the drawer a crack; commit slides the item in, and the drawer front thickens with what it holds.
- **Sediment** [4]. Archived things sink into layers at the bottom of the screen, oldest deepest. Rehearsal turns the item translucent and starts it sinking.
- **Flower press** [3]. The item is pressed flat between two boards and filed. It keeps its shape, thinner, forever.
- **Amber** [4]. Archive as preservation: the item is wrapped in amber, golden and unchangeable, and can be cracked out later.

## Send
- **Folded corner** [2]. The message's own corner is the control. Hover lifts the message and tilts it toward the recipient, whose avatar opens like a window; commit folds the message into a plane that flies there.
- **Mail slot** [3]. The recipient is a door. A dragged message makes the flap swing open as it nears; dropping it slides it through with a clack.
- **Capsule** [4]. The message rolls into a capsule and a pneumatic tube takes it with a thunk; delivered capsules sit in the outbox rack.
- **Bottle** [5]. For broadcasts to no one in particular: the message goes into a bottle and drifts off on the tide.

## Copy and duplicate
- **Carbon** [2]. Hover shows a blue carbon ghost offset under the content; commit peels the ghost off and it flies to a clip at the screen edge, which keeps a thumbnail.
- **Tracing paper** [3]. A translucent sheet slides over, the lines trace through, the sheet lifts off holding the copy.
- **Mitosis** [4]. Duplicate pinches the item in the middle until it divides in two (goo filter).

## Share
- **Ripple** [2]. Rings spread from the item while hovering the share control; recipients inside the rings light up.
- **Dealing** [3]. Copies fan out like cards dealt to each recipient, one per person.
- **Dandelion** [4]. The item becomes a seed head; holding blows the seeds, which drift to the people it's shared with.

## Export, print, download
- **Slot print** [3]. A slot under the document. Hover pushes a print halfway out in the target format: a sheet for PDF, a ledger strip for CSV, a photo print with a border for PNG. Commit completes it and it drops into a tray that is the download. See `examples/paper-export.html`.
- **Develop** [4]. For images: the print develops in a darkroom tray, emerging from white.
- **Mold** [4]. Content pours into a mold shaped like the output format and comes out solid.
- **Lower into the box** [2]. Download lowers the item on a string into a box at the bottom edge; the box fills and shows a count.

## Upload
- **Lift** [2]. Dropped files float up into the destination, which glows as they near.
- **Mouth** [3]. The drop zone is a slot that widens as a dragged file approaches and swallows it; the bulge travels while it uploads.
- **Planting** [4]. Files are seeds pressed into soil; uploading sprouts them; done means bloom; a failed upload wilts and can be replanted.

## Undo and redo
- **Reel** [2]. A small film reel. Hover shows the last change's previous state as a ghost; pressing plays it backwards. Dragging the reel scrubs history.
- **Footprints** [3]. Each change leaves a footprint along a path at the edge. Stepping back on one rewinds to it.
- **Ghost hands** [4]. Undo replays your own last gesture backwards with a faint ghost hand doing it.

## Publish
- **Stage** [3]. Content lifts onto a lit stage or sky layer; a halo stays while it's public. Unpublish lowers it into the wings.
- **Notice board** [2]. Publish pins the item to a public wall with a heavier pin.
- **Lantern** [4]. The post becomes a paper lantern that rises and joins others overhead.

## Schedule
- **River** [3]. Drag the item along a timeline river; it floats downstream to its slot and bobs there until its time.
- **Sundial** [4]. Rotate a sun around the item; its shadow points at the chosen hour.
- **Seed with a timer** [4]. Plant it now; it blooms at the scheduled time.

## Lock and unlock
- **Shackle** [1]. A physical padlock: hover drops the shackle a few pixels and desaturates the content; commit clicks it shut and presses a small mark into the surface.
- **Freeze** [3]. Frost creeps in from the edges of the target while hovering; commit freezes it read-only. Unlock is a warm hold that thaws it from one corner.
- **Glass dome** [3]. A dome lowers over the item. You can see it, you can't touch it.
- **Wax seal** [3]. Sealed documents can't be edited. Breaking the seal unlocks them and leaves the broken seal in the history.

## Pin
- **Push pin** [2]. The item stops drifting, its shadow tightens, the pin head stays visible.
- **Paperweight** [3]. A stone set on the item; it ignores scrolling and stays in place.
- **Magnet** [3]. The item snaps to a metal strip at the top and clicks.

## Favorite and like
- **Warmth** [2]. Hover warms the item with a golden light; liking leaves it warm; warmth fades over weeks unless revisited.
- **Dog-ear** [2]. Folding the corner marks the item; the fold is the mark.
- **Pressed flower** [4]. Favoriting presses a small flower into the corner; favorites collect into a herbarium.

## Rename and edit
- **Chalk** [3]. Names written in chalk. Hover glints the dust; click smudges the old name and the caret is a chalk stick.
- **Pencil** [2]. Editable text is graphite; read-only text is ink. The difference is the affordance.

## Close and dismiss
- **Fold away** [2]. The panel folds along a crease into the edge it came from.
- **Snuff** [3]. For notifications: a small candle; hover bends the flame; commit snuffs it with a curl of smoke.
- **Evaporate** [3]. Temporary messages are vapor; dismissing blows them away.

## Submit a form
- **Envelope** [3]. The form is a letter. The flap only closes when required lines have ink; empty required lines show a faint ghost of what goes there. Sealing submits and the letter drops into the slot.
- **Balance** [4]. A scale weighs completeness; when it balances, the submit weight can be set down.

## Add and create
- **Pad** [2]. A pad of blank cards; tearing off the top one creates a new item.
- **Pull a thread** [3]. A loose thread at the list's end; pulling it draws out a new item.

## Approve and reject
- **Stamps** [2]. Two stamps: a round seal for approve, a crossed bar for reject. Approve presses heavier; the impression is the record.
- **Scale** [4]. Evidence stacks on each side; the decision tips it.

## Sort and filter
- **Rake** [3]. Sorting rakes items into order like sand; the tines' marks show the order direction.
- **Sieve** [3]. Filtering shakes the list; non-matching items fall through a mesh into a tray below, still faintly visible. Clearing pours them back.

## Refresh and sync
- **Turn the page** [2]. Pull down; the top edge curls like a page; release turns it and new items arrive wet.
- **Snow globe** [4]. Shaking stirs the content; new items settle.
- **Tide** [4]. Sync is a tide that brings new items in and carries removed ones out.
